# B.Tech 5th Semester (3rd Year Autumn) — Open Elective Offerings

> **Institution:** Harcourt Butler Technical University (HBTU Kanpur)  
> **Scheme:** Inter-Departmental Open Elective Pool (CBCS)  
> **Target Audience:** All 3rd/4th Year B.Tech Students Across All Disciplines

---

## 🌐 Overview of Open Elective Courses

Open Electives allow undergraduate engineers at HBTU to diversify their technical and managerial skill sets by taking specialized courses offered by sister departments:

1. **Entrepreneurship Development (ED):** Business model canvas, startup financing, intellectual property rights, and pitching.
2. **Business Ethics & Professional Values (BEPV):** Moral reasoning, corporate social responsibility, whistleblower protection, and global engineering ethics.
3. **Industrial Management & Operations Research:** Linear programming, inventory control, PERT/CPM project scheduling, and queueing theory.
4. **Cyber Security & Intellectual Property Rights:** Cyber laws, digital forensics, IT Act 2000/2008, and patent filing procedures.
5. **Renewable Energy & Environmental Auditing:** Solar PV systems, wind turbines, carbon credits, and environmental impact assessments (EIA).

## 📚 Study Resources on StudyHub Unlocked Nexus

All open elective subjects feature unified note repositories. Regardless of which branch hosts the student, all verified lecture notes, question banks, and session-wise exam papers are pooled identically.
