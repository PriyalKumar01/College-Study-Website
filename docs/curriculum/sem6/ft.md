## 📌 Semester Overview

The **6th Semester (3rd Year Spring)** curriculum for **Food Technology** at HBTU Kanpur is engineered to provide rigorous theoretical foundations alongside hands-on laboratory experience. The courses align with AICTE and NBA accreditation criteria and prepare students for both high-impact campus placements (Tier 1 core engineering, product MNCs, FinTech) and competitive national examinations (GATE, ESE, CAT).

## 📚 Prescribed Courses & Curriculum

### 1. Meat, Fish & Poultry Processing

- **Course Category:** Professional Core / Core Engineering
- **Contact Hours:** 3 Lectures, 1 Tutorial, 0 Practicals (Credits: 4.0)
- **Examination Scheme:**
  - Continuous Internal Evaluation (Class Tests, Quizzes, Attendance): 20 Marks
  - Mid-Semester Examination: 30 Marks
  - End-Semester Autonomous Examination: 50 Marks
  - Total: 100 Marks
- **Key Modules Covered:**
