# B.Tech Semester Subject Filtering Architecture

## Overview
StudyHub Unlocked Nexus now features a high-performance cross-branch subject filter dropdown across all B.Tech semester landing views (Semesters 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8).

## Key Capabilities
- **Deduplication:** Aggregates unique subject names taught across all 13 engineering departments.
- **Typeahead Search:** Instant keystroke filtering matching YouTube and Google search UX.
- **Multi-Select & Reset:** Checkbox selection with 'Select All' and 'Reset' actions.
- **Persistence:** Preserves user selections in browser localStorage.
- **Direct Navigation:** Filtered cards offer direct links to individual branch pages and drive notes.
